Sukoon Salah - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Sukoon Salah ("the app") shows prayer times and silences your phone during congregational prayer. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we use

  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device location to calculate prayer times for where you are. Your coordinates are sent to a reverse-geocoding service (BigDataCloud) only to find the name of your city. If you search for a city, the text you type is sent to a geocoding service (Open-Meteo). These services may receive your IP address as part of normal internet requests.
  • Settings. Your chosen city, Jamaat times and preferences are stored only on your device.

We do not collect your name, email or phone number. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in the app. We do not sell your data.

Permissions

  • Location: to find prayer times.
  • Notifications and alarms: to alert you at prayer times.
  • Do Not Disturb access and vibrate: to silence or vibrate your phone during Jamaat and then restore it.
  • Boot completed: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service: to play the Azan sound.
  • Internet: to find your city name or search cities.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone except the location and search services named above, which are used only to provide the feature you requested.

Data deletion

Settings are stored only on your device. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
